=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
For 45 years, everyone thought this painting was destroyed.

Lady Jane Grey was Queen of England for just nine days in July 1553. On 12 February 1554, aged 16 or 17, she was beheaded at the Tower of London. A contemporary chronicle says that, blindfolded, she couldn't find the block and cried: "What shall I do? Where is it?"

In 1833 the French painter Paul Delaroche painted that moment, and at the Paris Salon of 1834 it caused a sensation. It was left to the National Gallery in London in 1902 and was kept at the Tate. On 7 January 1928 the Thames burst its banks and flooded the Tate's basement, and the painting was written off as lost.

In 1973 a young Tate curator, Christopher Johnstone, went looking for another painting lost in the flood, John Martin's Destruction of Pompeii and Herculaneum. Under a big table in the conservation studio he found rolls of canvas. The Martin was rolled up with the Delaroche, and Lady Jane was in almost pristine condition. Since 1975 she has been one of Britain's best-loved paintings.
