=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
There's sheet music painted on a sinner's backside in Hieronymus Bosch's Hell, the right panel of The Garden of Earthly Delights (c. 1490–1500). Around him, sinners are crucified on a harp and a lute, and a choir sings from the notes on his skin.

In February 2014, Amelia Hamrick, a 20-year-old music student at Oklahoma Christian University, spotted it one night, wrote it out in modern notation, recorded it and posted it online. The headlines called it "the 500-year-old butt song from Hell", and her university said she was the first to play it.

She wasn't. Scholars had written the notes out by 1961, and in 1978 Atrium Musicae, an early-music group from Madrid, recorded them. And nobody can really play it: the staff has no clef, so the pitch is a guess. Even Hamrick told the AP: "I think Bosch may have just been slapping notes up there to make it look like music."

P.S. Bosch painted the same kind of make-believe notes in the open music book under the lute.
