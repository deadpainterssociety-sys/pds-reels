=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
One strap on this dress nearly ended John Singer Sargent's career.

In 1884 Sargent, a rising young portrait painter in Paris, showed his portrait of Virginie Gautreau at the Salon, titled only "Madame ***". Everyone knew who she was: an American-born beauty famous for her pale, powdered skin. In his version, the jewelled right strap had slipped off her shoulder.

Critics mocked it. Her mother asked him to take it down. He refused. After the Salon he painted the strap back up onto her shoulder, but soon left Paris for London, taking the rejected portrait with him.

He kept it in his studio for more than thirty years. In 1916 he sold it to the Metropolitan Museum of Art: "I suppose it is the best thing I have done." He asked the museum to hide her name, so the world knows her as Madame X.

P.S. A photograph from 1884 still shows the fallen strap.
