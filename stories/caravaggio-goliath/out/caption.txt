=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
The severed head in Caravaggio's David with the Head of Goliath is the painter's own face.

In May 1606, in Rome, Caravaggio killed Ranuccio Tomassoni in a brawl. He was sentenced to death, a sentence anyone who recognised him could carry out. He fled to Naples, Malta and Sicily, and his paintings filled with severed heads.

Then he painted this: young David holding up Goliath's head, with Caravaggio's own features on the giant. On the sword are the letters H-AS OS, which many scholars read as "humilitas occidit superbiam": humility kills pride.

Historians think the painting was meant for Cardinal Scipione Borghese, the pope's nephew and the man who could win him a pardon. In the summer of 1610 Caravaggio sailed toward Rome, the pardon apparently within reach. He died on the way, at Porto Ercole, aged 38.

P.S. The painting is still in the Borghese collection, and the Italian state bought the gallery in 1902.
