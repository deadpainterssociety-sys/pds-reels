=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
Death is killing everyone in Pieter Bruegel's The Triumph of Death (c. 1562). An army of skeletons sweeps the land and rings the bell for the end of the world. A king's gold is looted, a cardinal is led away, a starving dog nibbles at a dead child.

Except in the bottom right corner. A young man plays the lute, a woman sings from a sheet of music, and they see only each other. Behind them, a skeleton is already playing along.

In 1960 Sylvia Plath published "Two Views of a Cadaver Room" (The Nation, 30 January 1960). It moves from a dissecting room to this very corner: "Two people only are blind to the carrion army". And then their sentence: "These Flemish lovers flourish; not for long."

P.S. The painting has been in the Prado since 1827.
