=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
There's a skull hidden in Holbein's The Ambassadors, in plain sight.

In London in 1533, Hans Holbein painted two young Frenchmen: Jean de Dinteville, ambassador to the court of Henry VIII, and his close friend Georges de Selve, Bishop of Lavaur. Both were under thirty. Around them: globes, scientific instruments, books, and a lute with one broken string.

At their feet floats a gray, stretched shape. Look up at the painting from the bottom right corner and it snaps into a skull. Holbein distorted it on purpose (a trick called anamorphosis), so it only appears from one angle.

"Memento mori", remember you will die, may have been Dinteville's motto. And in the top left corner, half hidden by the curtain, there's a tiny crucifix: death at their feet, and hope just out of sight.

P.S. The painting is over two metres wide, and the National Gallery bought it in 1890.
