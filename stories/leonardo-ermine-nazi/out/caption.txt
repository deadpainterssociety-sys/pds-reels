=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
For about a year, Leonardo's Lady with an Ermine hung in the office of Hans Frank, Hitler's lawyer and the Nazi governor of occupied Poland.

Leonardo painted Cecilia Gallerani around 1490, when she was about sixteen and the lover of Milan's ruler, Ludovico Sforza. In 1798 Prince Adam Czartoryski bought the painting in Italy. In 1830, as the Russian army invaded, his 84-year-old mother, Princess Izabela, hid it.

In 1939 the family walled it up at their palace in Sieniawa. The Nazis found it anyway and sent it to Berlin. Frank had it brought back to Kraków for his office in Wawel Castle, the royal castle from which he ran a reign of terror and took part in the mass murder of Jews.

In May 1945 American troops arrested Frank in Bavaria, and the painting was found at his country house. It came home to Kraków in April 1946. That October, Frank was hanged at Nuremberg.
