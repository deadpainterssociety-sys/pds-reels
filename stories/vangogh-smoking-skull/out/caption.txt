=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
Van Gogh painted this skeleton smoking, as a joke.

In January and February 1886 he was a student at the art academy in Antwerp. Drawing skeletons was a standard exercise there, to learn anatomy; painting them was not part of the lessons. So, somewhere between classes, he gave this one a lit cigarette.

The joke has a darker side. In a letter to his brother Theo that February, Vincent wrote that since the previous May he had not had a hot dinner more than perhaps six or seven times. He had been "smoking a great deal", because then you don't feel an empty stomach so much, and more and more of his teeth were breaking off. A doctor told him he absolutely must take better care of himself.

A few weeks later he left Antwerp for Paris. He kept smoking until his death in 1890.

It hangs in the Van Gogh Museum, in Amsterdam.

👁️ A joke about death, or about himself?
