=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
He never rode this horse across the Alps.

In May 1800 Napoleon led his army over the Great St Bernard Pass into Italy. Not on a stallion: he rode a mule, surer-footed on icy paths, led by a local guide, Pierre-Nicolas Dorsaz.

Then the King of Spain ordered a portrait of the crossing from Jacques-Louis David. Napoleon refused to pose; he reportedly asked to be shown "calm, on a fiery horse". David carved BONAPARTE into the rocks, next to Hannibal and Charlemagne, who had crossed the Alps before him. In the end, David's studio painted five versions.

Fifty years later, Paul Delaroche painted the crossing as it really happened: a man in a grey coat, on a mule, led through the snow (Walker Art Gallery, Liverpool).

So the rearing horse? Pure propaganda. This version, made for Milan in 1803, hangs in the Belvedere in Vienna.
