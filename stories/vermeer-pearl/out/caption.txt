=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
The most famous pearl in art isn't really there.

Vermeer painted Girl with a Pearl Earring around 1665 in Delft, and nobody knows who she was. It isn't a portrait: the Mauritshuis calls it a tronie, a painting of an imaginary figure, a girl in exotic dress with a turban and an "improbably large" pearl.

The museum says the pearl is too large to be real, probably an imitation, perhaps varnished glass. Astrophysicist Vincent Icke thinks it looks more like polished tin.

In 2018 the museum's scientists examined the painting with X-ray scanning and microscopes. They found tiny eyelashes, a green curtain that has faded away behind her, and no hook: nothing holds the pearl to her ear. Up close it is just two strokes of white paint, a thick dab on top and the reflection of her white collar below. Your eye does the rest.

P.S. In 1881 it sold at auction in The Hague for two guilders and thirty cents.

🖤 Did you ever notice there's no hook?
