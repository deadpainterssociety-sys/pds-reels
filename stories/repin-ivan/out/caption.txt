=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
This painting has been attacked twice.

Ilya Repin finished Ivan the Terrible and His Son Ivan in 1885: the tsar clutching the son he has just struck in a rage (most accounts say with his staff; historians still argue). Tsar Alexander III had it banned from exhibition for about three months.

In January 1913, Abram Balashov, a 29-year-old icon painter, slashed it three times with a knife, straight through the faces. Witnesses reported he shouted "Enough blood!". He was declared insane and sent to an asylum. The gallery's keeper reportedly threw himself under a train. Repin, then 68, came to Moscow to repaint the faces.

In May 2018 a man smashed the glass with a metal pole and tore the canvas in three places, saying Ivan never killed his son. After years of restoration it went back on display in December 2024, inside a bulletproof case.
