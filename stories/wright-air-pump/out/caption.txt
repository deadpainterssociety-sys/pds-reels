=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
This family is watching a bird suffocate, for science.

Joseph Wright of Derby painted An Experiment on a Bird in the Air Pump in 1768. A travelling lecturer pumps the air out of a glass globe, and inside it a rare white cockatoo struggles to breathe. The younger girl stares up at it; her elder sister can't bear to watch and hides her eyes while their father holds her. The only light is a candle hidden behind a glass bowl with a skull in it.

Look at the lecturer's hand: it is on the valve. He can let the air back in and revive the bird, or not. And he isn't looking at the bird. He looks straight out of the painting, at you, as if the decision were yours. Wright never shows us how it ends: the boy by the window may be lowering the empty cage for the bird, or hauling it back up.

It hangs in the National Gallery, in London.

P.S. The moon in the window is often read as a nod to the Lunar Society, the scientists who met at full moon so they could ride home by its light.
