=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
Our idea of "thumbs down" comes from this painting.

Jean-Léon Gérôme finished Pollice Verso in 1872. A gladiator stands over a beaten man in the Colosseum, and the crowd gives its verdict. Even the Vestal Virgins, Rome's sacred priestesses, lean over the wall with their thumbs turned down: kill him. Gérôme did his research: the gladiators' armour follows designs found at Pompeii.

The title is Latin for "with a turned thumb", words from Roman writers like Juvenal. But turned which way? Nobody knows. Up, down, sideways, hidden in the fist: the sources never say, and some scholars think a thumb pointing up meant death.

Over a century later, producers showed Ridley Scott a reproduction of this painting before he had even read a script. "That image spoke to me of the Roman Empire in all its glory and wickedness," he said. The film was Gladiator, and its thumbs come from Gérôme, not from Rome.

It hangs in the Phoenix Art Museum, in Phoenix, Arizona (bought in 1968).
