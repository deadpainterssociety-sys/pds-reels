=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
Pieter Bruegel painted this Flemish village in 1559, and every person in it is acting out a saying. By most counts there are more than a hundred.

A man in armour bangs his head against a brick wall. A woman ties the devil to a pillow. One man shears a sheep, the other a pig. Roses are thrown to the swine, the roof is tiled with tarts, the globe hangs upside down, and a monk ties a fake beard on the Lord.

And right in the middle, a woman in red pulls a blue cloak over her husband so he can't see a thing. In Bruegel's day a blue cloak meant deceit: she is cheating on him. That is the painting's old name: The Blue Cloak, or The Folly of the World.

P.S. His son, Pieter Brueghel the Younger, painted at least 16 copies of it.
