=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
Look at the letter in his hand. It's signed by the woman who just killed him.

On 13 July 1793, Jean-Paul Marat, the most radical journalist of the French Revolution, was working in his bath, as he often did because of a painful skin disease. Charlotte Corday, 24, talked her way in by offering him the names of traitors. He wrote them down. Then she stabbed him in the chest with a kitchen knife. Four days later she was guillotined.

The Convention asked Jacques-Louis David to paint him. David erased the skin disease, left the killer out of the room, and gave Marat the hanging arm of Michelangelo's dead Christ. On the block he wrote: "À Marat, David".

Corday appears only on the paper: "Du 13 juillet 1793. Marie Anne Charlotte Corday au citoyen Marat." The words below her name are David's invention.

🖤 Would you have noticed whose letter it was?
