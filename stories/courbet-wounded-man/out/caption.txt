=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
Courbet painted out the woman he loved, and gave himself a wound.

In 1844 the young Gustave Courbet painted himself asleep under a tree, with a woman resting on his shoulder. She was probably Virginie Binet, his companion for years and the mother of his son, born in 1847; she seems to be the woman in his Lovers in the Countryside, painted the same year.

Then she left him. Around 1854, ten years after he began the picture, Courbet painted over her. In her place: a sword propped against the tree, and a red stain of blood on his white shirt. The sleeping lover became The Wounded Man.

Courbet kept the painting until he died in 1877, and the French State bought it at auction afterwards. Today it hangs in the Musée d'Orsay.
