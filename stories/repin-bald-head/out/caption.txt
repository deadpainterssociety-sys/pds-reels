=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
This bald head was painted without its owner's consent.

In the middle of Ilya Repin's Reply of the Zaporozhian Cossacks (1880–1891), one Cossack turns his back to us: a shining bald head. It belonged to Georgy Alekseyev, an imperial court official, who refused to pose. The story goes that while he was distracted (Repin's friend, the historian Dmytro Yavornytsky, showed him a collection), Repin quickly sketched the back of his head. Repin is said to have exclaimed that a head like that takes centuries to make. When Alekseyev saw the finished painting, he recognised his own head, and he was not pleased.

Almost every face in that laughing crowd is a real person from Repin's life: Yavornytsky is the scribe, General Dragomirov is the ataman Sirko.
