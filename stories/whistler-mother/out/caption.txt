=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
Whistler's Mother only exists because a model didn't show up.

London, 1871: James McNeill Whistler was waiting for a 15-year-old girl to pose for another painting, Annabel Lee. She never came. So he asked his mother, Anna, then 67. Her letter remembers his words: "Mother I want you to stand for me!" She tried, but standing was too tiring, so he painted her sitting down.

He didn't even want it seen as a portrait. He called it Arrangement in Grey and Black, and later wrote: "what can or ought the public to care about the identity of the portrait?" He even pawned it; the French state bought it in 1891.

Then, in 1934, the US put her on a 3-cent Mother's Day stamp, cropped, with a vase of flowers added: "In memory and in honor of the mothers of America." The stand-in had become America's mother.

🖤 Would you have guessed she was a last-minute replacement?
