=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
Someone painted over a god in this Vermeer.

Vermeer painted Girl Reading a Letter at an Open Window in Delft around 1657–59. On the wall behind her hung a painting of Cupid. Decades after Vermeer died in 1675, someone covered Cupid with paint and left a blank wall. Nobody knows who, or why.

An X-ray found him in 1979, and for decades experts assumed Vermeer had changed his mind and hidden Cupid himself. Then in 2017 Dresden's restorers looked closer: under the cover-up, on top of Cupid's varnish, was a layer of dirt. Cupid had hung there in plain sight for decades. It wasn't Vermeer.

By 2021 the overpaint was gone. Cupid is back, stepping on a mask, a symbol of love without deceit. Experts think she's reading a love letter.

P.S. The same Cupid hangs on the wall in another Vermeer: A Young Woman Standing at a Virginal, in London's National Gallery.
