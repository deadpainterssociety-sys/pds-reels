=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
Her neck is the least strange thing in this painting.

Look at the baby: far too big for a baby, and lying on Mary's lap as if about to slide off. Look at the tiny saint with a scroll at lower right, St Jerome: as Gombrich put it, he hardly reaches the Madonna's knee. Now look beside him: one foot, and no saint. St Francis was supposed to stand there.

Parmigianino started this altarpiece in 1534 for a chapel in Parma. He died in 1540 with it still in his studio. When it went up on the altar two years later, an inscription was added on the step: "FATO PRAEVENTUS F. MAZZOLI PARMENSIS ABSOLVERE NEQUIVIT" — fate prevented Francesco Mazzola of Parma from completing it.

P.S. The column beside her may echo an old hymn to Mary: "Your neck is like a column."
