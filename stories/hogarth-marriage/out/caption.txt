=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
One man at this wedding deal will kill the groom.

William Hogarth painted The Marriage Settlement around 1743, the first of six scenes of Marriage A-la-Mode. On the right, a broke earl with gout points at his family tree, growing out of William the Conqueror; through the window his new house stands half built. Across the table the bride's father, a rich city alderman, reads the contract through his glasses. His cash goes to the earl; his daughter gets a title.

The groom admires himself in a mirror and takes snuff. The black patch on his neck may hide a syphilis sore. The bride turns away and slides her ring along a handkerchief, under a screaming Medusa. At their feet, two dogs chained together by the neck. And leaning in to whisper in her ear: the lawyer, Silvertongue.

Four scenes later, the groom catches them together and Silvertongue kills him with a sword. The lawyer is hanged, the bride poisons herself, and her father pulls the wedding ring off her dead finger.

All six paintings hang in the National Gallery, in London.
