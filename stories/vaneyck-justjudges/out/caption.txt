=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
On the night of 10 April 1934, a thief walked out of St Bavo's Cathedral in Ghent with the lower left panel of the Ghent Altarpiece, the van Eyck brothers' masterpiece finished in 1432. The panel showed the Just Judges on horseback, with St John the Baptist painted on its back.

The Bishop of Ghent then received a ransom demand: one million Belgian francs. To prove he had it, the thief returned St John the Baptist, left in a luggage locker at Brussels North station. Belgium refused to pay.

That November, a broker named Arsène Goedertier collapsed after a political speech. Dying, he told his lawyer that he alone knew where the panel was. In his papers: carbon copies of the ransom letters. But no panel.

In 1945 the restorer Jef Van der Veken painted a copy to fill the gap, and gave one judge the face of King Leopold III. The real Just Judges have never been found.
