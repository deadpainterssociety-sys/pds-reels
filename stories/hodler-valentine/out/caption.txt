=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
Watch Ferdinand Hodler paint the woman he loved as cancer takes her.

Valentine Godé-Darel was his lover and his model (he stayed married). In 1913 she had his daughter, and doctors found a gynaecological cancer. In 1914 came two operations, and radium treatment. Hodler sat by her bed in Vevey and kept painting: more than twenty paintings and many drawings of her decline.

On 24 January 1915 he painted her for the last time alive (Kunstmuseum Basel). She died the next day. On 26 January he painted her once more, laid out on her deathbed.

Then he painted Lake Geneva at sunset: nothing but long horizontal bands. For Hodler, the horizontal was the line of death.

P.S. He died three years later, in May 1918.
