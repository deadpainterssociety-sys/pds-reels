=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
This is Rudolf II, Holy Roman Emperor. Made of fruit.

Around 1591, his former court painter Giuseppe Arcimboldo, retired to Milan, sent him this portrait: a pear for a nose, apples for cheeks, cherries for lips, wheat and grapes for hair. He is dressed as Vertumnus, the Roman god of the seasons.

The emperor wasn't offended. He is said to have rewarded Arcimboldo with the title of count palatine.

In 1648, at the end of the Thirty Years' War, Swedish troops stormed Prague Castle and carted the treasures to Stockholm. On the transport list, item 351: "a face of fruit". It ended up in Swedish castles, and in the late 1800s an art historian dismissed it as of "inferior quality".

But look again: fruit, grain and flowers from every season, all ripe at once. The poem sent with it said the emperor ruled the seasons as he ruled the countries of the world.
