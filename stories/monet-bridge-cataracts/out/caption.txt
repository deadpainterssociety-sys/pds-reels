=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
Put Monet's paintings of his Japanese footbridge in order and you can watch his eyesight fail.

1899: his water garden at Giverny, cool greens, every leaf sharp (National Gallery of Art, Washington; National Gallery, London). In 1913 cataracts are clouding both his eyes; a London eye doctor, Richard Liebreich, urges surgery. Monet refuses. By 1918 colours no longer have the same intensity for him, and reds look muddy. He starts labelling his tubes of paint so he won't pick the wrong colour. In 1922 his right eye perceives only light. He keeps painting: this burning, almost abstract bridge dates from about then.

In early 1923 surgeons operate on his right eye. Now he can see the real colours of what he painted half-blind, and he begins to destroy canvases from those years. This one survived.
