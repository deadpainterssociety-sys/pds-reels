=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
Three people at this table are robbing the fourth.

Georges de La Tour painted The Cheat with the Ace of Diamonds in the 1630s. The victim is the rich, very young man on the right, holding his cards up with both hands: a card expert interviewed by the Louvre calls him completely exposed. The courtesan in the middle glances sideways at her partners, and her hand, a historian says, gives the order: your move. The servant, in a feathered turban and a low neckline that is hardly a servant's outfit, pulls every eye away from the man on the left. He looks straight at us, and behind his back he slips out an ace of diamonds. In prime, the Italian game they are playing, a 7, a 6 and an ace make the hand called 55.

Under his elbow: one gold coin, one silver, and the painter's signature, 'Georgius De La Tour fecit'. La Tour, a baker's son whose own son became a noble, left complaints in the archives: ruined fields, a beaten peasant. The Louvre's curator puts him on the cheat's side.
