=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
Mark Twain called this the vilest picture in the world.

In A Tramp Abroad (1880) he described Titian's Venus of Urbino, hanging in the Uffizi's Tribune, as "the foulest, the vilest, the obscenest picture the world possesses". It wasn't her nakedness, he wrote, but "the attitude of one of her arms and hand". And he was sure it had been painted for a bagnio, a brothel.

Now look closer. At her feet a little dog sleeps: for the Uffizi, a sign of fidelity in marriage. Behind her, two maids put away a gold and light-blue wedding dress in a chest. On the windowsill, a myrtle, the plant of Venus and lasting love.

The buyer, Guidobaldo della Rovere, simply called it "the nude woman". Many historians think it was made for a marriage, perhaps his own to Giulia Varano.

P.S. It reached Florence in 1633, in the dowry of Vittoria della Rovere, a bride.
