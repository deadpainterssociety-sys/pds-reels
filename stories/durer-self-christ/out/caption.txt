=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
These self-portraits show a goldsmith's son turning himself into Christ.

1484: still a boy, Albrecht Dürer draws himself in silverpoint from a mirror. Decades later he adds a note: "This I drew myself from a mirror in the year 1484, when I was still a child."
About 1491: a young journeyman, his hand pressed to his face.
1493: at 22 he paints himself holding a thistle, probably for Agnes Frey, the bride waiting for him in Nuremberg.
1498: back from his first trip to Italy, he wears silk gloves and Italian finery, dressed like a gentleman.
1500: at 28 he faces us head-on, symmetrical, his hand raised as if in blessing. No secular portrait looked like this: it was how painters showed Christ. Arrogance or blasphemy? Historians still argue.

From Venice in 1506 he wrote to his friend Pirckheimer that there he was a gentleman, at home only a parasite.

It hangs in the Alte Pinakothek, in Munich.
