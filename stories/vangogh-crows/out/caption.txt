=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
For decades, Wheatfield with Crows was sold as Vincent van Gogh's last painting: the storm, the black crows, then the gun.

He painted it in Auvers-sur-Oise, outside Paris, in July 1890. Around 10 July he wrote to his brother Theo and Theo's wife Jo that he had painted vast wheatfields under troubled skies, and had tried to express sadness, extreme loneliness. On 27 July he shot himself. He died two days later.

Irving Stone's novel Lust for Life and the 1956 Hollywood film turned the crows into his farewell. But his letters date the painting more than two weeks before his death. The Van Gogh Museum thinks his last work was probably Tree Roots, left unfinished.

P.S. In 1893 Theo's brother-in-law, Andries Bonger, wrote that the morning before his death Vincent had painted "an underwood, full of sun and life".
