=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
Every judge in Bruges had to look at this.

In 1498 Gerard David finished two huge panels for the Bruges town hall. The story comes from Herodotus: Sisamnes, a royal judge in Persia, took a bribe to give an unjust verdict, and King Cambyses had him killed and skinned. David paints him alive, tied to a table, while four men peel away his skin, and dresses the whole crowd like the people of Bruges in his own day.

Herodotus says the skin was cut into strips and laid over the judge's seat. Then the king named a new judge: the dead man's own son, told to remember what he was sitting on. Look at the top right corner of the panel: there he is, on his father's skin.

The aldermen of Bruges saw it every day, as a warning to stay honest.

P.S. In 2012 Yulia Tymoshenko's supporters showed a copy of this painting to the judge reviewing her case.
