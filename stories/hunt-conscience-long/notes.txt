Story: Hunt painted a kept woman "saved" by a song, in a rented room in St John's Wood, while trying to remake his model, the Chelsea barmaid Annie Miller, into his wife; she went her own way (Rossetti, Cremorne, Lord Ranelagh, married Captain Thomson in 1863), and the buyer Fairbairn had her painful face repainted.
