=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
This woman was painted for people to laugh at.

Quinten Massys painted her in Antwerp around 1513. Look at her horned headdress and her dress: the fashion of her youth, decades out of date. Look at her low, tight bodice, a scandal at her age, and the red rosebud in her hand. She was half of a pair: in the matching portrait of an old man, she holds the bud out to him, trying to win him over. The pair mocked old people who dressed and flirted as if they were still young.

Now look at her face. In 1989 a doctor writing in the British Medical Journal suggested she had Paget's disease, a real condition that thickens and deforms the bones; surgeon Michael Baum made a similar case in 2008. The National Gallery thinks she is more likely a figure from carnival folklore. But if the doctors are right, the joke was on a sick woman.

Centuries later, her face inspired John Tenniel's Duchess in Alice in Wonderland (1865).

It hangs in the National Gallery, in London.
