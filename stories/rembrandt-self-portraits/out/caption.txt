=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
Rembrandt painted his own face about 80 times in 40 years. Put them in order and you watch a life fall apart.

Around 1628 he is about 22, experimenting, most of his face in shadow. In 1640 he is rich and famous, dressed like a gentleman of the 1520s and posing like a Titian. Two years later his wife Saskia dies. By the early 1650s he has stopped paying for his big house in Amsterdam. In 1656 he declares himself insolvent; his art collection is auctioned and in 1658 the house goes too. That same year he paints himself enthroned, like a king (Frick Collection, New York).

In September 1668 his son Titus dies. In 1669 Rembrandt paints one of his last self-portraits, now in the National Gallery, London. The museum's experts found that his clasped hands first held a paintbrush. He painted it out. He died on 4 October 1669.
