=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
Look at the dead man's right hand. It's a slightly different colour from his arm, and there's a reason.

On 31 January 1632, in Amsterdam, Dr Nicolaes Tulp gave his public anatomy lesson on the body of Aris Kindt (Adriaen Adriaensz), a thief hanged that same day for stealing a winter coat. The surgeons hired a 25-year-old newcomer from Leiden to paint it: Rembrandt.

Tulp had himself shown opening the arm first, against the usual order, perhaps to echo the great anatomist Vesalius. But X-rays show that Rembrandt first painted Kindt's other arm ending in a stump. The Mauritshuis thinks he had lost the hand as punishment for an earlier crime, and that Tulp or Rembrandt found the stump too distracting, so a hand was painted on top. As the upper paint grew more transparent, the added hand began to show.

The museum's conclusion: the stump proves Rembrandt was in the room.

P.S. In 1828 the painting was about to be auctioned. King William I stopped the sale, and the Dutch state bought it for 32,000 guilders.

🖤 Would you have noticed the hand?
