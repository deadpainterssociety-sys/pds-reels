=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
People online swear this painting shows dinosaurs: long necks, tiny heads, walking through the hills with an army. Viral posts call it proof that humans lived beside them.

It's The Suicide of Saul, painted by Pieter Bruegel the Elder in 1562, a small oak panel (33.5 × 55 cm). The word "dinosaur" didn't exist until Richard Owen coined it in 1842. The animals are most likely camels, painted, it seems, without a real one to copy.

And while everyone hunts for dinosaurs, they miss the real subject. On a ledge at the far left, a few tiny figures: King Saul, beaten by the Philistines on Mount Gilboa. In 1 Samuel 31 he begs his armour-bearer to kill him; the man refuses, so Saul falls on his own sword, and the armour-bearer follows him. Bruegel painted both, and wrote his source on a stone at the bottom: "SAVL XXXI CAPIT", Saul, chapter 31.

A proud king shrunk to a speck while the army pours on through the mountains. The museum says it: the landscape is the main subject here.
