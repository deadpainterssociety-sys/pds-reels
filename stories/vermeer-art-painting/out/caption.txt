=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
Vermeer never sold this painting. Its last buyer was a dictator.

Johannes Vermeer painted The Art of Painting around 1666–68 and kept it until he died, even deep in debt. Look at the painter: his back turned, face hidden, in a slashed black doublet. Tradition says it's Vermeer himself. His model poses as Clio, the muse of history, with a laurel crown, a trumpet for fame and a book. Behind her, a map of the Netherlands with a crease right where the country was split in two. Above, a chandelier crowned by a double-headed eagle, read as the emblem of the Habsburgs. And on the map's edge, behind her collar, his signature.

In 1676 his widow handed it to her mother to keep it from creditors. In 1813 Count Czernin bought it in Vienna for 50 florins as a Pieter de Hooch, with a forged de Hooch signature. Only around 1860 did it get Vermeer's name back. In 1940 the Czernin family sold it to Adolf Hitler for the museum he planned in Linz. It spent the war in the Altaussee salt mine; American troops recovered it in 1945 and gave it to Austria. The heirs said the sale was forced. In 2011 Austria said no.

It hangs in the Kunsthistorisches Museum, in Vienna.
