=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
Monet painted his wife Camille just after she died.

Camille Doncieux had been his model since the 1860s. She is the woman in the green dress (1866) and the woman with the parasol (1875), walking on a windy hill with their son Jean. After their second son, Michel, was born in March 1878, her health got worse, and much of what Monet earned went on her care. She died in Vétheuil, on the Seine, on 5 September 1879. She was 32.

Monet sat by her bed and painted her face under the veil in quick strokes of violet, blue and white. Years later he confessed it to his friend Georges Clemenceau: at her bedside he caught himself, almost mechanically, noting the colours death had just put on her face. Blue, yellow, grey. His painter's eye wouldn't switch off, even then.

He kept the painting. It stayed in the family, came to the Louvre in 1963 and has hung in the Musée d'Orsay since 1986.
