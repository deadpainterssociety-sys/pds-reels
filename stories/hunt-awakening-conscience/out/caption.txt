=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
This woman's face is not the one William Holman Hunt first painted.

The Awakening Conscience (1853) looks at first like a young couple at the piano. Then look closer. She wears rings on every finger except the wedding finger: she is his mistress, in an apartment he pays for. Under the table a cat toys with a bird that is trying to escape. A glove lies cast off on the floor (Tate reads it as her fate: used, then discarded). The song he idly plays reminds her of the life she had before, and she rises from his lap toward the sunlit garden we see in the mirror. John Ruskin wrote to The Times that every object in the room becomes tragic if read rightly.

In 1854 The Athenaeum wrote that nobody 'could have conceived a more painful-looking face'. The buyer, Manchester industrialist Thomas Fairbairn, couldn't bear her expression and asked Hunt to soften it. So Hunt painted her face again. He retouched the picture in 1864 and again in 1886, and wrote his repairs on the canvas itself, in the top corners. The first face is lost.
