=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
Around 1641, Hendrick van Anthonissen painted the beach at Scheveningen, near The Hague: crowds on the sand, people on the dunes, all looking out to sea. At nothing, it seemed.

In 1873 the painting was given to the Fitzwilliam Museum in Cambridge. Nobody knew anything was missing. Then in 2014 student conservator Shan Kuang, at the Hamilton Kerr Institute, began removing the old varnish. A tiny man appeared, standing on the horizon, next to what looked like a sail.

It was a fin. Under a thick layer of later paint lay a stranded sperm whale, with the man standing on its back. Experts think a later owner had it painted out, in the 18th or 19th century. Maybe a dead animal seemed offensive. Maybe the picture simply sold better.

P.S. The museum notes there were many whale strandings on the Dutch coast in the first half of the 17th century.
