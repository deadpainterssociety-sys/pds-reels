=== INSTAGRAM (Reel) ===
To paint this drowning, Millais put a real woman in a bath.

In 1851 the young John Everett Millais spent five months painting the riverbank by the Hogsmill River in Surrey, from life.

The figure he painted back in his London studio. The model, Elizabeth Siddal, lay in a bath full of water, wearing an old embroidered dress Millais bought for four pounds. Oil lamps under the tub kept the water warm. One day they went out, and Millais was so absorbed he didn't notice. Siddal got very cold and fell ill. Her father ordered Millais to pay £50 in doctor's bills.

So the drowning girl is a real woman, lying in a bath that was slowly turning cold.

P.S. Because he painted the riverbank over five months, flowers that bloom at different times of year appear together.
