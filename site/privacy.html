Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This app is a private publishing tool used only by the Dead Painters Society team to upload our own videos to our own Instagram, TikTok and YouTube accounts. It is not offered to other users.

What we access. When we connect our own accounts, the platforms give the tool an access token with the permissions we approve (for example, uploading videos). We use it only to publish our own content and to read basic profile information about our own accounts. We do not access, collect or store data about any other person.

Storage. Tokens are stored encrypted as secrets in our private automation settings and are never shared or sold.

Deletion. We can revoke access at any time from each platform's settings, after which the tool can no longer act.

Contact. Message @thedeadpainterssociety on Instagram.
